## Design Context

### Users

LSCCC administrators and editorial staff managing news, media and public safety
information. Access is by invitation, with email and password login.

### Brand Personality

Authoritative, calm and clear. Staff should feel confident about each action.

### Aesthetic Direction

Extend the approved LSCCC website and admin workspace: navy, blue and gold,
Plus Jakarta Sans, the authentic centre emblem and original photographs.
Use restrained motion, readable forms and responsive layouts.

### Design Principles

- Preserve the approved visual language.
- Explain preview actions honestly; never imply email delivery or authentication.
- Make permissions and invitation states understandable.
- Keep keyboard access, mobile readability and proper code formatting central.
