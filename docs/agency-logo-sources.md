# Homepage agency emblems

These original agency images are stored locally in `public/images/agencies/` so
the response network does not depend on third-party image requests at runtime.
The artwork is displayed in its original colours and proportions.

The fire service image is the published government fire service crest. The main
agency website and its fire safety clearance image endpoint were unavailable
during retrieval; this source does not establish whether a newer crest is in use.
Replace it with an agency-supplied master asset if a different current emblem is
approved. The low-resolution LASEMA, LASAMBUS and LASTMA source images are kept
at modest display sizes rather than enlarged into illustrations.

Emblems identify the respective public agencies; no new agency marks were drawn.
